# Lexicon

The words language: every word Auburn shows, and the voice it speaks in. Words are written here as they are said; how a screen sets them, such as in capitals, is the screen's.

## Voices

- **Plain** — reality's voice. Every word below is plain, except the slogan.
- **House** — the corporation's voice. It speaks in the slogan.

## Lines

A line's parts are separated by " · ". Braces hold what the line is given.

| Line | Shown on |
|---|---|
| Last workout · {how long ago} · {gym} | The header's status |
| {n} in Train · {n} overdue | Rest at a glance's widget |
| Overdue · {groups} | Rest at a glance's widget |
| {n} goals | Goals' widget |
| Latest · {exercise} | Goals' widget |
| {n} days since last | Cardio's widget |
| Last run · {distance} km | Cardio's widget |
