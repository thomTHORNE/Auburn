# Touch language

How the hand acts on Auburn, and how Auburn answers it. Its values are in [basics.css](basics.css).

## Tap

Every key's tap area is at least 44 px.

## Press and hold

An action that cannot be taken back is pressed and held (*What cannot be undone takes effort*, in [Principles](../Principles.md)). Let go before it fills, and it is not taken. The [hold key](Visual/components/hold/hold.md) is how it looks; its fill and drain durations are in basics.css.
