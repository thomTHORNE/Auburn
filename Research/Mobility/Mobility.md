# Mobility

Mobility work, which Foundation says addresses weaknesses: how Auburn keeps it. Taken out of the spec so v0 can be hypertrophy and cardio. Seeded by [Origin](Origin.md).

## Settled

- **Mobility work addresses weaknesses**, as Foundation states.
- **A mobility exercise has no load and no sets.** It may take an optional grade: controlled, improved, or poor.
- **Mobility is done when the user feels the need.** Nothing rests or falls due.

## Open questions

- **One visit, two kinds.** Whether a workout can hold mobility and hypertrophy together, or each visit stays one kind, as Foundation's "never both" has it.
- **Goals.** Foundation sends weaknesses to mobility work, yet a mobility exercise serves no goal, and every goal needs a target the facts prove.
- **Routines.** Whether a routine holds mobility exercises, and with them whether a routine has a kind.
- **Foundation's terms.** Which of its mobility lines change: the exercise, the workout, the set and the grade.
- **The Notion history.** Whether its mobility workouts, left out of v0's import, are imported once mobility returns.

## Findings

- **Mobility never took part in the roll, rest, or progress.** Removing it leaves those features unchanged.

## Dependencies

- **Requires:** none.
- **Required by:** Routines, for mobility exercises in a routine.
