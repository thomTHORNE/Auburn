Status: Open

# Name

Whether "Auburn" can be held as a product name: registered, found in the stores, and given a domain.

## Settled

Nothing yet.

## Open questions

- **Trademark.** Whether "Auburn" can be registered for software (class 9) and fitness services (classes 41 and 44), with EUIPO or the national office.
- **Search.** How a listing is found under a common word. A descriptive store title, such as "Auburn — training by feel", is the usual answer.
- **Domain and handles.** The website's domain, Auburn's subreddit, and its social handles.

## Findings

- **"Auburn" is crowded.** Searches for it are led by Auburn University, the city of Auburn, Alabama, and the hair colour. No trademark register, domain, or subreddit name has been checked.

## Dependencies

- **Requires:** none.
- **Required by:** Distribution, Website, Forums — each carries the name.
