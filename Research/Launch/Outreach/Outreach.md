# Outreach

Asking three creators whose work shaped the developer's training to review Auburn.

## Settled

- **Three channels:** [GVS](https://www.youtube.com/@GVS), [Paulo Guga](https://www.youtube.com/@pauloguga), [Basement Bodybuilding](https://www.youtube.com/@BasementBodybuilding).
- **Each is asked for an honest review, free to criticise.** These channels are not expected to take a sponsorship, and none is offered. The creator is paid for their time and owes nothing in what they say.
- **Each review is also feedback.** A creator's criticism is weighed for what Auburn can do better, within what Foundation says Auburn is.
- **Each email is personal**, in this order: who the developer is; the offer of payment for the review; what Auburn is, and its guiding principles; a personal message on how the creator helped the developer's training; a closing line.
- **The personal messages:**
  - **Paulo Guga** — Auburn keeps its user honest with themselves, and gives a space where learning and experimentation happen, not mindless programming.
  - **Basement Bodybuilding** — the channel's advice not to force progressive overload and rep counting, and to focus instead on how a movement is executed and how the body moves through space, mended the developer's relationship with training.

  | The cost | The creator joins a test meant for the closest circle | None beyond the code |

## Findings

- **A sponsorship and a paid review are different purchases.** A sponsorship buys a placement and talking points the sponsor approves; the creator presents the product favourably, and criticism is not what it pays for. A paid review buys the creator's time and leaves the verdict to them.
- **A review is disclosed either way.** Under the FTC's Endorsement Guides, a free product given with the expectation of a review is a material connection the viewer is told about, even when the review is negative, and a payment on top is disclosed too. On YouTube the disclosure belongs in the video, not only its description, and the paid-promotion checkbox does not replace it. The guides were revised in 2023. EU and national rules are not yet looked up.
- **An offer a creator can half-accept is not refused whole.** A creator who takes no payment for reviews can still take the copy.
