# Launch

Everything Auburn needs to reach people besides the app itself — v0's testers, then v1's public: the name, distribution, copy protection, pricing, the website, the forums, and outreach to creators. Seeded by the conversation that opened it — see [Origin](Origin.md).

## Why together

Each topic waits on something different, and each is worth little alone. A pitch with no store page sends readers nowhere; a website with no price cannot sell; a creator sent an unprotected, unpriced app is sent the whole product. They are one launch, and they land in order.

## Settled

- **Auburn launches as it is.** It is what Foundation and the spec describe. Launch speaks to whoever trains this way, and never promises that Auburn fits another way.
- **Auburn runs on Android and iOS from v0.** v0's iPhone testers join through TestFlight.
- **v0 is the closed test Google Play requires.** See [Distribution](Distribution/Distribution.md).
- **Once promoted, Launch is a top-level `Launch/` beside `Spec/`.** It holds copy and business decisions, the pitch and the emails among them, not the reference for building Auburn.

## The voice

Launch copy speaks in the two layers Foundation's [Two realms](../../Lens/Foundation.md#two-realms) sets out: the corporation narrates, and a plain line says what the real app is. [Lore](../Lore/Lore.md) holds the corporation's voice — the slogan, the maxims, the references — and launch copy uses it as Lore has it.

## Order

The name is cleared first, since every piece carries it. Distribution and pricing follow, then the website, which needs both. The forums and outreach come last, once a reader can be sent to the stores and the website.

## Findings at the seams

- **Copy protection and pricing may be one mechanism.** If v1 is a free download with a one-time unlock, a reviewer's copy is a promo code for the unlock and needs no build of its own. Owned by Protection.
- **Every record stays on the phone, and that is a selling point.** Auburn sends nothing to its developer: Play's Data safety form stays short, and the website can say "your training never leaves your phone". Owned by Website.

## What would falsify it

Launch assumes enough people train the way Foundation describes to buy an app built for it. The v0 field test is the first evidence: testers who drop Auburn because it does not fit how they train say the audience is smaller than assumed.
