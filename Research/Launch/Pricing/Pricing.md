# Pricing

What Auburn costs, and how it is paid for.

## Settled

Nothing yet.

## Open questions

- **The model.** Paid upfront, free with a one-time unlock after a trial, or a subscription.
- **The price.** Not yet compared with apps like it.
- **Paying for what follows v1.** One-time revenue does not fund later development; whether later features are a paid add-on.

## Findings

- **Auburn costs nothing to run.** Its records stay on the phone, and intervals.icu is free to each user for personal use. There is no running cost to pass on.
- **A subscription is the fictional corporation's model.** Foundation's corporation builds "a loyal, dependent consumer base". A one-time price is the real Auburn refusing it, and the pitch's "invest in something that compounds" describes one.
- **The stores' free trials are for subscriptions.** Play's one-time products offer buy and rent, with pre-order and discount offers, and no free trial in Google's documentation. A trial before a one-time unlock is counted by Auburn: the date of first launch, kept on the phone. Changing the phone's clock defeats it.
- **Play's fees.** For installs and updates from 30 June 2026 in the EEA, the UK and the US: a 10% service fee plus a 5% billing fee on the first $1M of annual earnings. Elsewhere the older tiers hold: 15% to $1M, 30% above. The App Store's fees are not yet looked up.
