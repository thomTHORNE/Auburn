# Lore

The fiction Auburn's second realm is built from: the corporation, its voice, and where it reaches the user.

## Settled

- **Two realms, as Foundation states them.** The fiction never changes behaviour.
- **One slogan: "Introducing, the new You."** The boot shows it alone.
- **The references.** Severance for the culture: warm on the surface, uncanny in its practices, rules and language. Blade Runner and Total Recall for the body and identity as product. The visual language of all three is the strong inspiration.
- **Classical statues stand for the idealised figure.**
- **No line is lifted from the references.**
- **The maxims are the house doctrine, used across Auburn:** "Human, improved.", "Built better. By you.", "Engineered to endure.", "Strength is a protocol.", "Advancement is mandatory.", "Obsolescence is optional.", "Flesh: the first machine."
- **A maxim is the corporation's doctrine, never a statement about the user's training.** It never names a muscle group, an exercise, or something to do now.
- **No maxim appears while a set is being logged.**

## Open questions

- Plain labels, fiction second: whether every label stays plain and the fiction lives only around it.
- The dose: whether the uncanny builds with time in use, and how.
- The house vocabulary: the corporation's words for Auburn's mechanics.
- Maxims: whether each is fixed to one moment or drawn from a pool. Candidate pairings, and candidates for the set:

  | Moment | Maxim |
  |---|---|
  | Overdue Log asks why a group wasn't trained | Deviation is noted. |
  | A goal goes active and its target locks | Commitments are binding. |
  | A modality is rolled | Assignments are final. |
  | The rest screen | Rest is maintenance. |
  | A goal is reached | Human, improved. |
  | A goal is abandoned and its reason kept | Obsolescence is optional. |
  | An empty state, before any workout | Built better. By you. |

  Unplaced candidate: "The old You is retired."
- The founder: whether the statues are a person, a doctrine, or the standard body "the new You" is shaped toward.
- Commendations: uncanny badges that praise compliance. They record only facts that already happened and never ask the user back.
- In-world paperwork for system surfaces: intake, memos, the release of records.
- Sound's part in the voice.
- Outward pieces: the subreddit pitch, ads, a corporate site.
- The mark: a statue clipped into the three pillars, whose boot animation zooms out from a full-width bust to the whole figure inside one pillar. Still open: the statue's source, and whether the final figure is wholly enclosed or fills the pillar's height.

## Findings

- Several Foundation mechanics already read as corporate policy without changing them.

## Dependencies

Requires nothing. Required by any feature, screen or sound that carries in-world text.
