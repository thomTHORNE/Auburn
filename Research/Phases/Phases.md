# Phases

A planned period of training with an intent of its own and the outcome it expects. A bulk: calories go up and strength is expected to rise. A cut: calories come down, and weight and strength are expected to drop a little.

## Settled

- A phase covers training as a whole. Hypertrophy, mobility, and cardio are never phases of their own.
- A phase is something the user plans. Auburn never starts or ends one.

## Open questions

- **Optional or continuous.** Whether phases come and go, or every stretch of training sits in one.
- **What a phase holds.** Name, dates, intent, and which expectations — strength, body weight, anything else.
- **Calories and body weight.** Whether Auburn records them, or the phase states the intent and the numbers live elsewhere.
- **What a phase plans.** Whether it carries a workout plan, or only the intent.
- **How it ends.** On a date, or when the user ends it.

## Findings

- **A phase changes how progress reads.** A strength drop during a cut is what the phase expected, not a setback. The log shows the drop either way; only the phase says whether it was expected.
