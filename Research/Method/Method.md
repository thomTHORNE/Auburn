# Method

The mechanics of how the user trains, as run in Notion (Training '26) since December 2025, and what Auburn takes from them. The why is Foundation's. Seeded by [Origin](Origin.md).

## Settled

- **Rest is per goal**: days since the latest set of any of its exercises, except AMAP exercises, which never reset it.
- **The overdue log** is standalone. It is tied to no goal, exercise, or workout: an entry is an answer to why training did not happen, kept so slack can be looked at over time.
- **Modalities**: slow and controlled; counted, to test strength; by feel. One is rolled on commit.

## Open questions

- **Rest bands.** Notion uses ≤2 days red (repair), 3 blue (maybe), 4 green (train), 5+ orange (train). Whether these carry over, and where the overdue limit sits.
- **The overdue prompt.** When it appears, and whether answering is required before anything else.
- **Rolling.** Whether the three modalities are equally likely, whether a goal or exercise can exclude one, and whether a roll can be re-rolled.
- **Progression.** How many ideal sets lift a Yielding hold, and whether Light, Acceptable, and Ideal carry rules of their own.
- **Gyms.** Equipment differs by gym. Whether Auburn knows which gym a workout is at, and whether a goal's alternate exercises are how it adapts.
- **Progress view.** Notion charts each exercise's ideal load over time. What Auburn shows.
- **Mobility workouts.** Logged without sets, following a fixed routine. How Auburn records them.

## Findings

- **Reps were dropped in practice.** 30 of 850 sets carry a rep count, none since August 2026.
- **Built, then abandoned:** fixed splits and previous/next links (unused since May 2026), drop-set notation, intended reps, ignore-tracking, and activity counts. Each is structure that did not earn its place.
- **AMAP moved from goal to exercise.** Older Notion boards marked whole goals "[AMAP]"; the rule now marks exercises.
