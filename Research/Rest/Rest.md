# Rest

Rest redefined: what it is kept for, what it counts, how its scale is set, and what of muscle groups, the muscle figure and the overdue log stands with it. The spec's Rest, Muscle Groups, Muscle Figure and Overdue Log hold until this topic replaces them.

## Settled

- **Rest is kept per muscle group.** The muscle is what tires, not the exercise.
- **Rest counts the days since a muscle was last a main mover.** Supporting work is shown beside it with its date, and never counted.
- **A muscle that is only ever supporting keeps a last date and no rest.** It is never owed. Making it a main mover somewhere is the user's choice.
- **Nothing is summed into a verdict.** A routine shows its exercises; an exercise shows its main movers' rest. A summed meter would decide the day for the user.
- **Rest reads in four steps: shouldn't, maybe, should, overdue.**
- **Each muscle's steps come from its own rhythm,** accepted on trust until the Lab's sketch gives its verdict: Should from the middle of its last six gaps as a main mover, Maybe a day earlier, Overdue at half again as long. Under three gaps, fixed steps hold. A gap over twice the usual marks the muscle "back after N days".
- **Any muscle's steps can be set by hand,** and handed back to its rhythm later. Auburn never sets them.
- **Overdue is the fourth step of the meter, and asks nothing.** The overdue log leaves v0 for Ideas.md; Foundation's "Rest has a limit… Auburn asks why" changes when this topic promotes.
- **v0 shows muscles and rest as lists. The muscle figure comes in v1.**
- **An exercise with no main mover resets nothing.** The AMAP flag leaves for Ideas.md.

## Open questions

- **Naming muscles on an exercise.** How an exercise names its main movers and supporting muscles.
- **The muscle figure in v1.** What it lights and tints under main movers and supporting muscles.
- **Cardio.** Whether cardio's rest follows the same steps and rhythm.

## Findings

- **In Notion, rest was a computed property of an exercise.** A commit reset its meter to 0, and it filled by the day. It was glanced at before every session to choose the day's routine.
- **Exercises left behind filled toward overdue, and that pulled training back to them,** even as a cherry-picked session.
- **One scale for every exercise did not fit.** Legs need more rest than chest or lats.
- **No fixed scale fits over time.** After a layoff a muscle needs far longer; at the most consistent, some could be trained almost daily.
- **Auburn is not bound to Notion's shape.** Exercises share muscles, a fact Notion's pages could not hold.
- **The least rested muscle deciding an exercise's rest lets a supporting muscle block it.** Curls the day before made rows read "shouldn't". This is why only main movers count.
- **Rest per muscle covers stand-ins.** An alternate or another gym's version resets the same muscle.
- **Rhythm knows how often the user trained, not how recovered they are.** It follows a gradual change in consistency; a comeback is flagged, not guessed at.
- **Rhythm lags a change of pace** by up to six gaps, the price of shrugging off one-off gaps.
- **Rhythm was hard to grasp on paper.** Fixed steps set by hand are the fallback if it does not land in use.
