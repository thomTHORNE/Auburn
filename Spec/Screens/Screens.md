# Screens

Every screen of Auburn, each built from the [languages](../../Lens/Lens.md#terms) and carrying the behaviour [Features](../Features/) specifies. The screens are agreed one at a time, as the project's visual design process sets out.

| # | Screen | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | [Boot](00-boot/00-boot.md) | The brand, for the moment the app takes to open | Agreed |
| 01 | [Navigation](01-navigation/01-navigation.md) | How the screens connect, and what opening Auburn shows | Agreed |
| 02 | Rest at a glance | Every tracked group's rest in a list, with cardio | Not yet specified |
| 04 | Starting a workout | Choosing the gym, then a routine or none | Not yet specified |
| 05 | Committing an exercise | The exercises offered here, and swapping for an alternate | Not yet specified |
| 06 | Logging a set | Load, grade, marking a test and its reps, and the notes | Not yet specified |
| 07 | Routines | The routines and their groups, and creating or editing one | Not yet specified |
| 08 | Past workouts | Past workouts, and one opened for corrections | Not yet specified |
| 09 | Exercises | The list, and one exercise with its notes, progress, past sets, and its main movers' rest | Not yet specified |
| 10 | Creating or editing an exercise | Its fields, and naming its muscle groups | Not yet specified |
| 11 | Goals | Goals, and one goal with its progress | Not yet specified |
| 12 | A muscle group | Its rest, its rhythm, its last supporting day, and steps set by hand | Not yet specified |
| 13 | Splits and gyms | Managing splits and gyms | Not yet specified |
| 15 | Settings | The theme mode, sound and its volume, the home layout, and credits | Not yet specified |
| 16 | Power down | The tube switching off while the power-down plays | Not yet specified |
