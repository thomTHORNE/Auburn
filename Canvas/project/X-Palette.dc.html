PALETTE · RAMPS AND ROLES · EXPLORATION
Each ramp is built around one colour, which stands in it exactly: its hue at seven steps of lightness (L), computed in OKLCH; chroma (C) eases where the screen cannot show more. Contrast is measured against the ground and the list sheet: 4.5 reads as body text, 3 as large text. Nothing here is a basic: a colour kept from this board joins the palette by name first.
