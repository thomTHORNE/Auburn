# Muscle figure regions — sketch

Throwaway code. The question, pass condition, and verdict live in [`Lab/Log.md`](../Log.md).

A front and a back figure on an ember plate, one region per muscle group, under the CRT and glass shader at strength 0.6, glass LOW, travel SMALL. The regions are placeholders: rough anatomical shapes in [`assets/figure.svg`](assets/figure.svg), one path per muscle group per view, generated by [`tool/gen_figure.py`](tool/gen_figure.py). Only their size and placement are being judged, not the art.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Exercise (top left) | Cycles placeholder exercises. The groups each one works light up in Cyan White. The muscle groups are guesses, not your own ticks. |
| `REST` | Tints every group with a random rest band — repair, maybe, train, overdue. Tapping again re-rolls the bands. |
| `FRONT ▸ BACK` | Turns the figure. |
| `TAP TEST` | Names each of the 20 groups once, in random order, turning the figure when needed. Tap the named group; the line under the buttons scores hits and lists misses as asked→tapped. |

Outside the test, tapping a region outlines it and prints its name.

## Running it

From this folder, with the Fold connected and the app on the **cover screen**:

```
flutter run --release -d <device id>
```

1. Run `TAP TEST` one-handed, holding the phone as you would between sets. Screenshot the score.
2. Step through the exercises on both sides of the figure: are the lit groups found at a glance?
3. Turn `REST` on a few times: do the four band colours read on the ember plate, and does the figure still look engraved?

## Known limits

- The shader's curvature moves the picture slightly at the edges; taps land on the undistorted layout. Near the plate's edges the two can differ by a few pixels.
- Placeholder shapes are simpler and larger than real muscles. Small groups on the traced art may be harder to hit than here.
